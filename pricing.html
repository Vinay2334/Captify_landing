How billing works

Paid plans are billed monthly through Dodo Payments. Checkout opens on Dodo's hosted page — Captify never sees your card details. Your extension account is matched automatically by email, and minutes reset on the 1st of each month. Cancel anytime; you keep the plan until the end of the billing period, then drop back to Starter.

Questions

What counts as a subtitle minute?

One minute of audio Captify captions for you, billed in whole seconds. Silent stretches and failed sessions bill 0.

How does the extension know I paid?

Sign in on this page before checking out and the plan is linked to your account automatically — you can pay with any email. Dodo notifies our server, which moves your account to the new plan, usually within a minute. Reopen the extension menu to see it.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrading applies immediately; downgrading takes effect at the next billing date.
